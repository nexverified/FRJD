Website & enquiry terms

These terms explain the enquiry stage. A written quotation and agreed service terms govern any subsequent order.

Enquiries are not orders

Submitting a product link, quote request or contact form does not place an order, reserve goods, authorize payment or guarantee service availability. FRJD reviews the request before confirming what it can arrange.

Information and quotations

Provide accurate product specifications, quantities, destination details and contact information. Listing prices and availability can change. Only an agreed written quotation establishes the proposed service scope, charges, currency, payment terms and validity period.

Purchasing and inspection

Agree the supplier, specifications, inspection coverage and handling instructions before purchasing. Inspection findings depend on the agreed scope and cannot establish compliance or the absence of every defect. Specialist testing or certification requires separate confirmation.

Freight and customs

Transport options depend on cargo, documentation, destination and carrier acceptance. Transit dates are estimates unless expressly agreed otherwise. Confirm import responsibilities, restricted goods, insurance options and any duties or taxes within the written quotation.

Cancellations and claims

Cancellation, return, refund, loss and damage arrangements depend on the agreed order and supplier or carrier terms. Request these terms before payment. Report discrepancies promptly with your order reference and supporting evidence.

Use of the website

Do not submit unlawful requests, misleading information, malicious links or personal information you are not authorized to share. Automated product retrieval and live tracking are not offered by this release.

Contact

Direct questions to FRJD through the contact form. These website terms do not replace the commercial agreement for a particular shipment or override mandatory rights under applicable law.
