You find the product. We coordinate the purchase.

Send a listing from 1688, Taobao, Alibaba or your supplier. FRJD reviews the specifications, communicates with the supplier and confirms purchasing arrangements before you commit.

01Send the specification

Share your product link, quantity, variants, packaging requirements and destination. A photo or written specification can help when there is no listing.

02Review the supplier and quotation

We clarify availability, minimum order quantity, unit price, domestic delivery and service charges. A listing price alone is not a final quotation.

03Approve before purchasing

Review the written scope and payment instructions. Purchasing starts only after the commercial terms are agreed. Supplier changes require your approval.
