Questions before you start.

Understand the process, the costs and what needs to be confirmed for your order.

Can I send a 1688 or Taobao link?

Yes. Submit the product link with the quantity, destination and your requirements. Listings are reviewed manually for V1; we do not display unverified product prices or availability.

What if I do not have a product link?

Use the quote form to describe the product, specification and intended use. Include a product name and any supplier or model reference you have.

Is submitting a request an order?

No. A request starts a conversation. It does not authorize purchasing, create a shipment or take payment. Review and approve the written quotation first.

How is the total cost calculated?

The quotation separates goods, domestic China delivery, purchasing services, inspection, handling, packing, freight and applicable customs charges. Only the agreed quotation establishes the charges.

Can you combine purchases from different suppliers?

Request consolidation in your enquiry. FRJD will confirm receiving instructions, compatibility, packing requirements and charges before the suppliers dispatch.

Do you inspect every item?

The inspection scope is agreed per order. Visual checks, sampling, full inspection and specialist testing have different coverage and costs. Do not assume a listing includes laboratory testing or certification.

Can I track a shipment here?

Automatic tracking is not connected in this V1. Submit your shipment reference on the tracking page to request a manual update. No simulated shipment events are displayed.

Are batteries or branded goods accepted?

Acceptance depends on the goods, documentation, route and destination. Declare batteries, liquids, powders and regulated or branded goods before purchasing. See Restricted Items.

When will I hear back?

The team reviews requests and contacts you using the details you provide. A response-time guarantee is not currently published. Keep your request reference for follow-up.
