Privacy Notice

How this website handles your enquiry information.

Information you provide

The forms collect your name, company (if provided), email or WhatsApp number, product or shipment details, destination and message. Please do not submit payment-card details, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Purpose and recipients

FRJD uses this information to review and respond to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and coordinate requested services. Relevant product and shipment details may need to be shared with suppliers or logistics providers to assess your request.

Storage and service providers

Website enquiries are stored in the website’s server-side database. Hosting and database providers process information to operate the service. Procurement and delivery coordination may involve processing in China and the relevant destination countries.

Retention and requests

Enquiry information is kept while needed for follow-up, service delivery and applicable recordkeeping. You can ask about access, correction or deletion through the contact form, identifying the original request reference. FRJD may need to verify that the request relates to you.

Browser storage

The website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. A submission identifier and a private access token are held in page memory while you submit a procurement request. The private access token is returned in a link fragment that may remain in your browser history when you open or save the link. Treat the complete link as confidential: anyone with it can view and act on your request. Your form content is not saved to browser local storage by this website.

Service information

FRJD refers to Shenzhen Furui Jieda International Logistics Co., Ltd. Use the contact page for privacy enquiries. Hosting providers may process technical connection information for security and service operation.
