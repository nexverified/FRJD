A quotation you can check.

There is no universal rate for a product link. Your goods, service requirements, packed dimensions and destination determine the cost.

What goes into the quotation

Goods

Supplier price, quantity, variants and currency.

China delivery

Transport from the supplier to the confirmed receiving location.

Purchasing service

The agreed fee for purchasing and supplier coordination.

Inspection

The inspection method, coverage and evidence you request.

Handling & packing

Receiving, storage, consolidation, repacking or special preparation.

International freight

Transport mode, packed weight and dimensions, destination and surcharges.

Customs & delivery

Applicable duties, taxes, clearance and final delivery within the agreed scope.

No fixed service percentage, free storage period or live freight rate is promised on this website. Request a written quotation showing inclusions, exclusions, currency and validity.

Why packed dimensions matter

Carriers may charge by actual weight or volumetric weight. The applicable calculation depends on the carrier and transport service. Ask for the final packed measurements and charging basis before approving freight.

When can a quotation change?

A change in product specification, quantity, packaging, dimensions, supplier availability or destination may require a revised quotation. Confirm the final scope before payment.
