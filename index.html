Can I send a 1688 or Taobao link?

Yes. Submit the product link with the quantity, destination and your requirements. Listings are reviewed manually for V1; we do not display unverified product prices or availability.

What if I do not have a product link?

Use the quote form to describe the product, specification and intended use. Include a product name and any supplier or model reference you have.

Is submitting a request an order?

No. A request starts a conversation. It does not authorize purchasing, create a shipment or take payment. Review and approve the written quotation first.

How is the total cost calculated?

The quotation separates goods, domestic China delivery, purchasing services, inspection, handling, packing, freight and applicable customs charges. Only the agreed quotation establishes the charges.
