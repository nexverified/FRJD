Quality inspection

Check the goods before they travel.

Agree the inspection scope before the supplier dispatches. Receiving checks, photographs and more detailed inspection answer different questions; we help you specify what needs to be checked.

01Define acceptance criteria

Tell us the required model, quantity, colour, dimensions, packaging and any known defects to look for. Request a sample before a larger purchase when appropriate.

02Agree the inspection method

Basic visual checks are different from sampling, functional testing or laboratory certification. Sampling levels and specialist tests must be confirmed in the quotation.

03Review findings before release

Request photographs and the agreed inspection record. If a discrepancy is found, discuss supplier remediation before international shipment. Inspection cannot guarantee that every defect is detected.
