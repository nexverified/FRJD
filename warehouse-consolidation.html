Warehouse & consolidation

Bring separate purchases into one shipment.

Coordinate supplier deliveries, receiving, consolidation and packing through FRJD. We confirm the receiving address and handling instructions for your order before goods are sent.

01Coordinate incoming goods

Use the order reference on every carton and share supplier dispatch details. Do not send goods to an address taken from a photograph or an old invoice.

02Confirm packing requirements

Tell us which items can be combined, which packaging must stay intact and whether additional protection is needed. Storage and handling charges are confirmed in writing.

03Measure the finished shipment

Final packed weight and dimensions determine the shipping quotation. Consolidation may reduce packaging volume, but savings depend on the goods and the selected route.
