Start with the information you have.

Share a listing or describe the product. Include the quantity, variants, destination, delivery requirements and any target price. Missing product information is clarified during review.

Agree the scope before committing.

The written quotation should specify the supplier and goods, purchasing fee, domestic delivery, inspection, storage or handling, packing and international delivery. Ask about exclusions and the quotation validity period.

Keep each handover identifiable.

Use the FRJD request or order reference when contacting the team. Obtain the confirmed receiving address and carton instructions before a supplier dispatches goods.

Review the finished shipment.

Confirm the inspection outcome, packed dimensions, freight option and required documents. Tracking availability depends on the carrier and shipment. Manual updates can be requested through this website.
