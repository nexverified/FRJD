Declare the goods before purchasing.

Some goods cannot be shipped; others require documentation, specific packaging or specialist handling. Acceptance must be confirmed for your route.

Do not submit unlawful shipments

FRJD does not accept requests involving illegal goods, counterfeit products, narcotics, weapons or goods whose purchase or export/import would violate applicable law.

Always flag these categories

  • Batteries, battery-powered products and magnets.
  • Liquids, powders, chemicals, aerosols and flammable substances.
  • Food, medicines, supplements, cosmetics and medical products.
  • Plants, seeds, animal products and temperature-sensitive goods.
  • Branded goods, regulated electronics and products requiring destination approvals.
  • Oversized, fragile, high-value or specially controlled items.

What to provide

Include the product specification, composition, intended use, packaging, quantity and destination. Safety data sheets, battery test summaries, licences or other documents may be required depending on the product and route.

This list is not exhaustive and is not an acceptance decision. Do not purchase or dispatch regulated goods until the specific requirements are confirmed.
